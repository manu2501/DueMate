# Changelog

What changed in each version of DueMate, newest first. The app shows this file under
Settings › What's new, so write for the people using it: short, plain lines.

Versions are `major.minor.patch+build`, the same as `version:` in `pubspec.yaml` and
`AppConstants.appVersion` / `appBuildNumber` (a test keeps the three in step). Start a new
`## version · date` section for each release, with `### New`, `### Improved` and `### Fixed` as needed.

## 1.1.0+2 · 2026-11-13

### New
- Track what you spend, by category, against a monthly budget, with spending trends.
- Repeating monthly expenses such as rent and subscriptions.
- Your own EMIs and loans are added to Expenses each month; shared ones go to that person's ledger.
- Mark any EMI month as paid or unpaid, pay ahead, or change one month's amount.
- Set a "return by" date when you lend money, and see who is overdue.
- Reminders the day before an EMI or a return-by date.
- See how often someone repays on time.
- App Lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
- Hide amounts when using the app in public.
- UPI QR codes and pay links (for ₹).
- Wallet as a payment method.
- Restore everything from a backup file.
- Lending trends.
- Version and What's new in Settings.

### Improved
- A calmer design: whole rupees on screens, fewer buttons, and empty sections stay hidden.
- Home starts with who owes what, then your spending and the next EMI.
- Shorter forms: optional details are folded away until you need them.
- Deleting an EMI or loan asks first and removes the payments it recorded.

### Fixed
- Changing an EMI's start date or paid count now updates the payments already recorded.

## 1.0.0+1 · 2026-11-12

### New
- Record money you give and receive, person by person.
- People list with balances, search and filters; add people from your contacts.
- Card EMIs and loans shared with someone.
- Activity with filters, and statements as PDF or CSV.
- Payment reminders on WhatsApp.
- Light and dark themes, and a choice of currency.
- Back up to a file. Everything stays on your phone.
